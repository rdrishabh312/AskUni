## 🧐 What is this project? (In simple words)
Imagine if **ChatGPT** and your **University Website** had a baby. That's **AskUni**.

It's a smart chatbot that knows *everything* about your college. Instead of searching through 100 boring web pages to find out when fees are due, you just ask AskUni: *"When is the fee deadline?"* and boom—it tells you instantly.

---

## 🛠️ What did I use to build it? (The Tech Stack)
Teachers love knowing "what's under the hood". Here is the simple version:

### 1. The Brain 🧠 (Backend)
- **Python**: The main coding language I used. It's powerful and popular.
- **FastAPI**: This helps the app talk to the internet quickly.
- **Ollama & Llama 3.2**: This is the **AI Magic**. It's an open-source AI brain (like a mini-ChatGPT) that runs *right clearly on the computer*. We don't need to pay OpenAI!

### 2. The Look & Feel 🎨 (Frontend)
- **Next.js & React**: Modern tools to make websites look cool and work fast. Facebook and Instagram use these!
- **TailwindCSS**: Used to style everything so it looks professional (glass effects, nice colors).

### 3. The Research Assistant 🕵️‍♂️ (Web Search)
- **DuckDuckGo Search**: The app can actually search the internet to find the latest info about the university.

## 🚀 Top 5 Coolest Features
1. **Instant Answers**: No waiting. It replies in seconds.

## ❓ Common Questions (Cheat Sheet)

**Q: Is this just ChatGPT wrapper?**
**A:** "No! Standard ChatGPT doesn't know about *our* specific college updates today. My app searches the web for current info and *then* uses AI to explain it."

**Q: What if the internet acts up?**
**A:** "The AI model runs locally on the computer, so basic chat still works! But for fresh web info, it needs internet."

**Q: Can it hallucinate (lie)?**
**A:** "AI can sometimes make mistakes, but since my app searches for real sources first, it's much more accurate than just guessing."
